Who it's for

Campus facilities teams paying to light and cool rooms nobody is using.

A pre-trained local AI module that reuses existing CCTV to count people and drive lighting and HVAC. No new hardware, no video stored.

The challenge

Saving energy without watching people.

Problem 1 · Energy

Empty rooms waste power.

Lights and air-conditioning keep running in rooms nobody is using, and today's fixes fall short:

  • Motion sensors are unreliable: they switch lights off on people sitting still while studying
  • New "smart" sensors are costly: device cost plus installation in every room

Problem 2 · Privacy

Cameras feel like surveillance.

Cameras can see occupancy well, but putting them in classrooms raises serious privacy concerns. A solution has to save energy without becoming a monitoring system.

The goal

Design an AI system that cuts wasted energy in shared campus spaces, without new hardware, while protecting the privacy of the people in them.

System design

From camera feed to energy decision, entirely on-device.

The key idea: campuses already have CCTV. LX-Sense is 100% software. A pre-trained AI model runs on a low-power device such as a Raspberry Pi, counts people in the live feed in real time, and passes on only an anonymous head count for energy decisions.

Robust over perfect.

Rather than chasing high-precision recognition, the decision logic was designed to be reliable in dynamic real-world rooms, where people come and go, lighting changes and furniture moves, and to fail safely rather than switch lights off on a full class. Unlike motion sensors, a people count doesn't drop to zero when students sit still.

Design priorities

  • Robustness in changing, crowded spaces
  • Reliability so energy control never disrupts a class
  • Ethical deployment in shared environments

Privacy by design

Built to control energy, not to watch people.

  1. On-device

    Processing stays local

    All processing happens on the local device; video is never recorded or sent anywhere.

  2. Minimal data

    Nothing identifiable stored

    Only an anonymous people count is passed on; no images or personal data are stored.

  3. Purpose-limited

    Energy, not surveillance

    The decision logic is limited to lighting and power control, by design.

Impact & recognition

  • Honorable Mention (รางวัลชมเชย) in the energy-conservation poster contest, KMUTT Energy Saving Day 2025.
  • Energy: potential to significantly reduce wasted electricity.
  • Environment: supports KMUTT's green campus goals by lowering the carbon footprint.
  • Experience: lights would no longer switch off on students sitting still.

What I learned

  • Privacy shapes the architecture. It decided where processing runs and what data exists at all.
  • Think at the system level. Real-world AI is about the whole loop, not just the model.
  • Balance performance with responsibility. Sustainability tech still has to earn people's trust.
